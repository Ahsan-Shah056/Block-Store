## 🚀 Getting Started

To use BlockMarket, you need a Web3 wallet installed in your browser.

### 1. Install MetaMask 🦊

Download and install the [MetaMask extension](https://metamask.io/) for your browser (Chrome, Firefox, Brave, or Edge).

### 2. Create Your Wallet 💳

Follow the prompts to set up your digital wallet. This will be your personal bank for storing Ethereum (ETH) and BlockMarket Tokens (BMT).

### 3. Connect to BlockMarket 🔗

Click the **"Connect Wallet"** button at the top right of the website. Approve the connection in MetaMask, and you're ready to go!

## 🛒 Buyer Interface

The Buyer Dashboard is your hub for shopping, tracking orders, and managing your rewards.

### 🏠 Home Page (Shop)

- **Hero Section**: View platform stats like Total Products, Orders, and Active Sellers.
- **Search & Filter**:
  - **Search Bar**: Type keywords to find specific items.
  - **Category Filter**: Dropdown to select Electronics, Clothing, Books, etc.
  - **Price Sort**: Organize products by Low-to-High or High-to-Low.
  - **Price Range Slider**: Filter products within your budget (ETH).
- **Product Grid**: Browse product cards showing the image, name, price (ETH & USD), stock, and seller rating.

### 🛍️ Shopping Cart

Access by clicking the **Cart Icon** in the navbar.

- **Review Items**: See product images, names, and prices.
- **Adjust Quantity**: Use `+` and `-` buttons to change how many items you want.
- **Remove Items**: Click the trash icon 🗑️ to remove a product.
- **Checkout**: See the total cost (including VIP discounts if applicable) and proceed to secure payment.

### 📦 My Orders Tab

Track all your purchases in one place.

- **Order Status**:
  - 🟡 **Pending**: Order placed, funds in Escrow.
  - 🔵 **Shipped**: Seller has sent the item.
  - 🟢 **Delivered**: You have confirmed receipt.
  - 🔴 **Disputed**: Issue raised, funds frozen.
- **Actions**:
  - **Confirm Delivery**: Click this ONLY when you receive the item. This releases funds to the seller.
  - **Raise Dispute**: If there's an issue, click this to freeze funds and alert support.
  - **Leave Review**: After delivery, rate the product to earn BMT rewards.

## 🏪 Seller Interface

Turn your passion into profit with our comprehensive Seller Dashboard.

### 📊 Dashboard Overview

- **Welcome Header**: Shows your Store Name and current **Seller Level** (e.g., New Seller, Verified).
- **Stats Cards**:
  - 📦 **Total Products**: Number of active listings.
  - 🛒 **Total Sales**: Number of completed orders.
  - 💰 **Total Earnings**: Cumulative ETH earned.
  - 💼 **Available to Withdraw**: Funds released from Escrow, ready for your wallet.

### 📦 "My Products" Tab

Manage your inventory efficiently.

- **Add New Product**: Click the button to list an item.
  - _Details_: Name, Price (ETH), Category, Stock, Description, Image URL.
- **Product List**: View all your listed items in a table.
- **Edit/Deactivate**: Update prices/stock or remove items from the market.

### 🚚 "Orders to Ship" Tab

Fulfill orders to get paid.

- **Pending Orders**: See new orders waiting for shipment.
- **Order Details**: View Product Name, Quantity, Buyer Address, and Your Earnings.
- **Action**: Click **"Mark as Shipped"** once you have sent the physical item. This updates the status for the buyer.

### 💸 Withdrawal

- Located in the **Stats Section**.
- When funds are "Available to Withdraw" (after buyer confirmation), click the **"Withdraw"** button.
- The ETH is instantly transferred to your connected MetaMask wallet.

## 🌟 Key Features & Tabs

### 🛡️ Secure Escrow System

> "Trust, but verify."
> We protect both parties. Buyers know their money is safe until they get their goods, and sellers know the money is there before they ship.

### ⚖️ Dispute Resolution

Problems happen. Our decentralized dispute system allows buyers to pause payments if something goes wrong, ensuring fair mediation.

### 💎 BlockMarket Token (BMT)

Our native loyalty token. Earn BMT by simply using the platform!

- **Buy a Product**: +10 BMT
- **Leave a Review**: +5 BMT
- _Future Utility: Use BMT for discounts and premium features!_

### 🏆 Seller Reputation System

Sellers earn reputation points for every successful sale.

- **Level 1**: New Seller
- **Level 2**: Verified Seller
- **Level 3**: Top Rated
- _Higher levels build trust and attract more buyers!_
